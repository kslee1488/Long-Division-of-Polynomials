The Four-Step Cycle: Divide, Multiply, Subtract, Bring Down

1. Divide

Divide the leading term of the current dividend by the leading term of the divisor:

$$q_k = \frac{\text{Lead}(\text{Current})}{\text{Lead}(D(x))}$$
2. Multiply

Multiply the new quotient term $q_k$ by every term in the divisor $D(x)$:

$$\text{Product} = q_k \cdot D(x)$$
3. Subtract

Subtract the product from the aligned dividend terms above (flipping every sign):

$$\text{Top} - (\text{Product}) = \text{Top} + (-\text{Product})$$
4. Bring Down

Bring down the next term of $P(x)$ and repeat until $\deg(R) < \deg(D)$:

$$\frac{P(x)}{D(x)} = Q(x) + \frac{R(x)}{D(x)}$$
